# Bodycam aimbot for PC: bones, smooth targeting, and FOV controls

In BODYCAM, aiming is a major part of every encounter. Its close-range firefights, unpredictable engagements and tactical environments make aiming one of the most interesting aspects of the game to explore.

The Bodycam aimbot feature collection brings together several targeting options in one interface. From head and chest targeting to FOV controls, aim lines and additional aiming functions, there is a substantial selection of features to discover.

This guide takes a closer look at the available Bodycam aimbot options, explains the different feature categories and covers the additional aiming and weapon settings included in the package.

## Aimbot, FOV, and extended targeting options

The aiming tab lists head and chest bones, an FOV Circle, Aim Line, Silent Aim, Magic Bullets, Instant Kill, Instant Aim, and No Aim Restrictions. The FOV circle marks the active field of view for target selection, while the aim line gives a visible guide from your reticle toward the chosen bone. Silent Aim and the instant-kill family are separate toggles in the same category rather than distinct products.

- Aimbot — Head / Chest
- FOV Circle
- Aim Line
- Silent Aim
- Magic Bullets
- Instant Kill
- Instant Aim
- No Aim Restrictions

## Weapon tools, ESP, and system requirements

Weapon-related settings—No Recoil, Rapid Fire, Full Auto, Unlimited Ammo, and God Mode—live in their own category so they are not confused with bone selection.
